# Journal Targeting — DigiUMKM

## Scope positioning
DigiUMKM sits at the intersection of:
- Technology management and innovation.
- Digital transformation of MSMEs.
- Gamification and technology adoption.
- Digital payment continuance.
- Design Science Research / information systems.

The strongest positioning is not “a new QRIS payment system.” It is “a gamified technology-management intervention designed and empirically evaluated to support sustainable QRIS use among rural MSMEs.”

## Candidate journals to screen

### 1. Journal of Technology Management and Innovation (JOTMI)
- Why it may fit: technology management, innovation, adoption and management implications.
- Potential positioning: design and evaluation of a technology-enabled innovation for rural MSMEs.
- Official site: https://www.jotmi.org/
- Author/submission information: https://www.jotmi.org/index.php/GT/about/submissions
- Previous checks in this project indicated no submission fee/APC and English-language submissions; reconfirm current policy before submission.
- Important fit issue: the manuscript must make a substantive technology-management/innovation contribution, not merely describe an app.

### 2. International Journal of Technology Management
- Why it may fit: technology strategy, innovation management, and technology adoption at organizational level.
- Publisher page: https://www.inderscience.com/jhome.php?jcode=ijtm
- Check current Scopus source status, CiteScore quartile by relevant ASJC category, article fit, and fees directly before submission.

### 3. Asian Journal of Technology Innovation
- Why it may fit: technology innovation and adoption, including emerging-economy contexts.
- Publisher page: https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/rajt20
- Previous publisher information surfaced a 3% acceptance rate; treat as a highly ambitious target, not an “easy” journal.
- Hybrid model: confirm current open-access charges and subscription-route terms with publisher.

### 4. Technology Analysis & Strategic Management
- Why it may fit: technology strategy, innovation systems, and policy/management implications.
- Publisher page: https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/ctas20
- More suitable if the article develops broader implications for technology strategy or innovation policy, not only an app usability study.

## Quartile verification checklist
Quartiles change by year and subject category. Before selecting a target:
1. Open Scopus Sources and search the exact journal title or ISSN.
2. Confirm the source is active and not discontinued.
3. Open the current CiteScore metrics.
4. Record CiteScore year and percentile/quartile for the relevant ASJC category, preferably `Management of Technology and Innovation`, `Information Systems`, or another category justified by the paper.
5. Do not substitute SJR quartile for CiteScore quartile.
6. Distinguish `Best Quartile` from the quartile in the specific category.
7. Save a dated screenshot/PDF of the Scopus source record for institutional documentation.

## Submission-cost checklist
- Check mandatory submission fee.
- Check mandatory publication/page fee.
- Check optional gold open-access APC.
- Check whether subscription publication has no APC.
- Check waiver eligibility and taxes.
- Verify on the publisher's official page immediately before submission.

## Recommendation
First screen JOTMI for scope fit and cost. Keep International Journal of Technology Management as an alternative. Treat Asian Journal of Technology Innovation as a stretch target due to its reported selectivity. Do not promise acceptance or a quartile without checking the live Scopus record at submission time.

## Manuscript quality gate before submission
- Clear novelty beyond “gamification is fun.”
- A well-justified DSR artifact and design principles.
- Rigorous usability and empirical evaluation.
- Evidence of actual or longitudinal QRIS-use behavior where possible.
- Clear rural MSME context and technology-management implications.
- Validated measures and transparent methods.
- English editing and journal-specific formatting.
- Ethics approval/consent and data protection statement.
